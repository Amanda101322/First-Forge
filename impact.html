trying to leave things a little better

Impact

Small things count too. This is where I keep track of the ways I learn, contribute, teach, and show up.

COMPUTER SCHOOL

Teaching basic computing

I've volunteered at a computer school where I help teach basic computing skills.

It's one thing to know how to use a computer. It's another thing to help someone else feel like they can use one too.

SPEAK & SHARE

Friday conversations

I also organize "Speak and Share Your Opinions" talks on Fridays at my computer school.

A little space for people to speak, listen, disagree, think, and actually have conversations.

COMMUNITY

Showing up

I MC and usher at events when I can, and I'm part of my church choir.

not everything has to be a grand project

MY TAKE

Impact doesn't always look impressive from the outside.

Sometimes it looks like teaching someone how to use a computer. Sometimes it looks like making space for a conversation. Sometimes it just means showing up and helping where you can.
